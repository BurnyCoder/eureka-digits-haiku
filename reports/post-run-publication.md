This bookkeeping and the separate artifact-verification work are outside the bounded trial and do not change its completion status.

The pilot fixture forwards test features through an evaluation-only network during checkpoint reload. It stores and scores validation predictions for the pilot; test labels do not enter training, hyperparameter selection or the pilot metric. Consequently, earlier prose saying that the pilot never accesses test features is too strong. The split is an example partition, and writer-level generalization has not been established.

The controller was repaired during this development trial. Restarts retained consumed active time and CLI calls. Downtime while repairing the harness is outside the registered active-time clock. The final deadline stopped the owned CLI process; partial output and all raw transcripts remain local. This trial is evidence of specific successes and failures during development, not a frozen-version benchmark or a comparison of agent capabilities.
